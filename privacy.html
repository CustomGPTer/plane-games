Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026. This policy covers the Plane Games app for Android (package uk.jj.planegames) and this website. Plane Games is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom.

The short version

  • Plane Games has no accounts and no servers. The developer receives nothing from your phone.
  • There is no advertising and no analytics in the app.
  • Your name, your badge, your games and your tallies are kept on your phone.
  • When you link with another phone, that phone receives your name, your badge and your moves, and nothing else.
  • The only time the app uses the internet is to ask whether there is a newer version.
  • The app does not read your location, your contacts, your microphone, your photos or your messages.

What stays on your phone

Plane Games keeps the following in its private storage:

  • The name and badge of each player you have added on the phone.
  • The result of each finished game: which game, who it was against, who won, how long it took and when.
  • Games in progress, so that they can be carried on.
  • The moves of the last forty finished games, so that they can be watched again.
  • Flight Cups, your settings, and a made-up identifier for this phone and for each phone you have linked with, so that the phones know each other next time.

If your phone's own backup to your Google account is on, Android may include this in it, as it does for most apps.

What the other phone receives

Linking is direct, from one phone to the other, by Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. Nothing passes through the internet or through any server. The other phone receives the name and badge you chose, the made-up identifier of your phone, the games you propose and the moves you make, and any of the short preset messages you send. It keeps your name, badge and the results of your games in its own tallies.

While you are hosting or joining, phones nearby that are also looking can see the name you chose. Pick a first name or a nickname.

Sending the app to another phone uses your phone's own sharing, such as Quick Share or Bluetooth. Plane Games hands over a copy of its app file and nothing else.

Plane Games does not ask for your contacts, your microphone, your photos, your messages or access to all your files.

Services the app contacts

There is one. To look for a newer version, the app asks GitHub for the number of the latest release of Plane Games (api.github.com). GitHub sees your internet address and the technical details any web server sees, and handles them under its own privacy policy. The request carries the app's version number and nothing about you, your phone's contents or your games. Without a connection the check does nothing.

The link between two phones uses Nearby Connections, a part of Google Play services that is already on the phone. Plane Games uses it without the internet.

Keeping and deleting your data

Everything is kept until you remove it. Settings has Wipe all tallies and Forget linked phones. A player can be removed from the phone, which deletes their tallies. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it has stored. A backup file you saved is yours and stays wherever you put it until you delete it.

The developer holds no copy of anything and so has nothing to delete or hand over.

Your rights

Data protection law in the United Kingdom and the European Union gives you rights over personal data that an organisation holds about you. The developer holds none from the app. Everything is on your own phone, where you can see it, export it as a backup file, and delete it.

This website

This site is a set of plain pages hosted by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other companies; its fonts are served from the site itself. GitHub, as the host, receives your internet address and browser details when you load a page, and when you download the app file, under its own privacy policy.

Children

Plane Games has no chat beyond a fixed set of short messages, no accounts, no purchases and no adverts, and it cannot connect to anyone who is not within a few seats. It collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. Changes that matter will be listed in the release notes.
