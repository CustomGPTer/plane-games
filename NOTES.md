First release.

Twenty games for two Android phones or one, made to work with no internet.

- Fourteen games for two: 9-ball pool, 8-ball pool, chess, draughts, backgammon, dominoes, battleships, Connect 4, reversi, ludo, snakes and ladders, dots and boxes, hangman, and noughts and crosses
- Six games to play alone: solitaire, FreeCell, Spider, minesweeper, sudoku and 2048
- Two linked phones, one phone passed between you, or the computer at five levels
- Linking with no internet, in flight mode or out of it, and a game that carries on after the link drops
- Tallies against each person, the Flight Cup, replays, head starts, clocks for chess and draughts
- More than one player on a phone, a backup file, and a notice when a newer version is ready

Full notes: https://customgpter.github.io/plane-games/releases.html
