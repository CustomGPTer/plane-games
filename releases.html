Release notes

What changed in each version of Plane Games. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.2 22 November 2026Latest

  • Pool has a new table: polished wood rails, lit cloth and leather pockets. A potted ball drops over the lip of the pocket and runs down a rail at the side, where the balls stack in the order they went in.
  • Pool has a new cue that draws back and follows through, a power bar, a wheel for fine aim and a bigger ball for setting spin. The top of the screen shows the balls each player still needs.
  • Computer players have names that go with their level: Alfie at Beginner, David at Easy, James at Medium, Victoria at Hard and Arthur at Expert, with four more at each level for bigger tables. Your tallies carry over.
  • Texas Hold'em and three card brag say what each player did in the words used at the table. In Hold'em: posts the blind, checks, calls, bets, raises, folds, goes all in, with the flop, the turn and the river named and a showdown that says who shows what. In brag: bets blind, looks, raises, pays to see. A History button lists the hand so far.
  • Pontoon says each card the bank twists and what it makes.
  • A new result screen in every game, with the winner's picture crowned. At the card tables the winning cards light up, the chips slide to the winner and a banner says who won what and with which hand, such as “David wins 240 with a full house”.
  • Installs over 1.1 and keeps your games, tallies and settings.

Version 1.1 21 November 2026

  • Six new games, twenty-six in all: Texas Hold'em and three card brag (two to six seats), Snap, Code breaker, Pontoon and 120 chess puzzles.
  • Up to four phones on one link, with computer players to fill seats, for ludo, snakes and ladders, dots and boxes, dominoes, Texas Hold'em and three card brag.
  • Private cards and tiles between linked phones.
  • Settings for computer speed and effects.
  • The home screen is in tabs (Board, Cards, Arcade, Puzzles) with Favourites and Recent. Part-played games can be continued or removed.
  • The computer's move is lit on the board, with a line saying what happened and a Show again button.
  • Battleships explosions, tumbling dice, moving pieces in every game and win celebrations.
  • The About screen now links to the website's licences page.

Version 1.0 19 November 2026

  • Fourteen games for two: 9-ball pool, 8-ball pool, chess, draughts, backgammon, dominoes, battleships, Connect 4, reversi, ludo, snakes and ladders, dots and boxes, hangman, and noughts and crosses.
  • Six games to play alone: solitaire, FreeCell, Spider, minesweeper, sudoku and 2048.
  • Three ways to play: two linked phones, one phone passed between you, or against the computer at five levels. Ludo, snakes and ladders, dominoes and dots and boxes take up to four players on one phone.
  • Linking two Android phones with no internet, in flight mode or out of it, by tapping a name or scanning a code, with a number check the first time.
  • A game that is saved on both phones and carries on from the same move after the link drops or a phone is put away.
  • Dice rolls and shuffles made by both phones together over the link, and battleship fleets and hangman words locked in before play starts.
  • Pool played by touch, with spin, a power bar, ball in hand, three aiming-line lengths, upright or sideways, and a left-handed layout. 8-ball by UK pub rules or with ball in hand.
  • Chess with the Stockfish engine, hints, undo, and a head start of a pawn, knight, rook or queen. Draughts by English or international rules, with a head start of one or two men.
  • Clocks of five, ten or thirty minutes each for chess and draughts.
  • Tallies against each person and each computer level, by game, with recent results, and personal bests for the games played alone.
  • The Flight Cup: a contest with one person over any games, first to three, five or seven wins.
  • Replays of the last forty finished games.
  • More than one player on a phone, each with their own name, badge and tallies.
  • A sudoku puzzle book of 240 numbered puzzles, and solitaire deals that can always be won.
  • Sending the app to another phone by Quick Share or Bluetooth, and a code that opens the download page.
  • Night, cabin and day colours, three board finishes, sounds off by default with vibration, short preset messages between linked phones, a battery saver, and a layout for tablets.
  • A backup file for every player's tallies and cups, to keep or to move to another phone.
  • How-to-play cards for every game, and a notice when a newer version is ready.
  • Android only, and two phones at most on one link.
  • Linking needs Google Play services on both phones. Without it the app still plays on one phone, against the computer and alone.
  • On Android 12 and older, Location must be switched on while the phones find each other.
  • Clocks are for chess and draughts only. Other games are not timed.
  • Pool has no shot clock and does not ask you to call a pocket.
  • Hints are given in chess, draughts, backgammon, Connect 4, reversi, dots and boxes, and noughts and crosses, and only against the computer or on one phone.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.2 is called PlaneGames.apk. It is 4.9 MB (4,877,480 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

caf6815d95ce85443509223802cc821047e7b99e73e390ea08482eb5b88b964f

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile PlaneGames.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.
