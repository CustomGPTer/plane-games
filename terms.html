Plane Games is an independent app made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom. It is a personal project, offered free of charge. It is not connected with any airline, with Google, or with the maker of any other game or app. The games in it are traditional games; names such as Connect 4 are used only to say which game is meant.

You may install Plane Games and use it free of charge on as many devices as you like, and pass the app file on to other people free of charge. The app has a button for doing exactly that.

Using it fairly

Plane Games is for playing games with people who want to play. Only link with, and only send the app to, someone who has agreed. The name you choose is shown to phones nearby while you link; choose one that is fit to be seen.

The tallies are a friendly record kept on each phone. They are not a ranking and prove nothing. Plane Games must not be used for gambling.

On aircraft

Rules about phones, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi on aircraft differ between airlines and countries and can change during a flight. Whether you may use Plane Games on board, and when, is for the airline and its crew to say. Follow their instructions at all times. The same applies anywhere else that restricts phones or radios.

What it cannot promise

Plane Games cannot promise that two particular phones will link, that a link will hold, or how far apart the phones can be. That depends on the phones, their settings, and what is between them. The computer players are there for practice and make no claim to play perfectly. Deals marked as winnable were each played out to a win when the app was made; that does not mean a win is easy to find.
